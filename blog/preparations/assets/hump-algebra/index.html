The humps are exp(−|x − a|²/(2s²)), with height one before multiplication by their coefficient. Positive coefficients sum to one in each factor; these are not probability-density normalizations. Every panel uses the same square [−2.5, 2.5]² with equal axis scales. Smooth colors sample a 181 × 181 grid; contours occur at 20%, 40%, 60%, and 80% of the displayed colorbar. The residual compares the n²-term Gaussian expansion with pointwise multiplication on a separate 17 × 17 grid.
