Pₚ(x, y) = ((xᵖ + yᵖ)/2)¹/ᵖ, with P₀ = √(xy). Each update uses the same old pair: (xₙ₊₁, yₙ₊₁) = (Pₚ(xₙ, yₙ), Pq(xₙ, yₙ)). In exact arithmetic, the limit lies between min(xₙ, yₙ) and max(xₙ, yₙ), so the midpoint error is at most the bracket half-width. The displayed bracket uses ordinary floating-point arithmetic, not certified interval arithmetic; its half-width excludes rounding error. A collapsed or stalled floating-point bracket is reported separately. Selected points stop at relative half-width 10⁻¹² or 64 updates.
