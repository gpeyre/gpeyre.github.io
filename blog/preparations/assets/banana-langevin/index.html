Numerical method and what this comparison does not claim

A deterministic, mass-conserving finite-volume approximation solves ∂tρ = Δρ + div(ρ∇Vb) in the physical (x,y) plane. Scharfetter–Gummel fluxes preserve the sampled target exactly; alternating implicit steps preserve positivity. The 121 × 321 grid covers [−6,6] × [−6,26], with reflecting boundaries and time step 0.0125. Saved densities and diagnostics are linearly interpolated in time. The plots crop the remote tails; the KL uses the full computational grid. These are finite-domain numerical results, not a measurement or proof of the optimal log-Sobolev constant. The displayed bounds concern the full-space target and are proved in the article.

For every bend, the initial law is the same shear of N((−1.5,0),I); its full-space relative entropy is exactly 1.125. The common density colors use a square-root intensity scale for legible tails. Equal spatial units and the color scale do not change with time or bending. The entropy axis is logarithmic; values below 10−9 are clipped for display only.
