A book I am delighted to recommend: [*Tout comprendre (ou presque) sur l’intelligence artificielle*](https://www.cnrseditions.fr/catalogue/societe/tout-comprendre-ou-presque-sur-l-intelligence-artificielle/), by [Olivier Cappé](https://www.cnrseditions.fr/auteur/olivier-cappe/) and [Claire Marc](https://www.cnrseditions.fr/auteur/claire-marc/), forthcoming from CNRS Éditions on 10 April 2025.

I have the pleasure of sharing my office with Olivier, so I was able to witness the creation of this fantastic book.

Olivier is a CNRS senior researcher in the computer science department at ENS, and Claire is a science communicator and graphic facilitator. Together, they have written an illustrated introduction for a French-speaking general audience.

The book explains where AI comes from, how its methods work, what resources they require, and what they can and cannot do. Image generation and robotics are among the examples, with attention to the limitations of these technologies.

I particularly appreciate the effort to help readers form their own opinions. Understanding the methods makes it easier to take part in discussions about AI. I warmly recommend the book, including to readers without a technical background.
