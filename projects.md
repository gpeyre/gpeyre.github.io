Current projects
----------------

* [WOLF](../wolf/): an ERC project using optimal-transport flows to learn multi-omics dynamics.
* [Automath](https://www.math.ens.psl.eu/la-recherche/automath/): a Paris community exploring machine learning and proof assistants for mathematics.
* [ENS Center for Data Science](https://csd.ens.psl.eu/): a hub for data-science research, seminars and courses at ENS.
* [ELLIS Unit Paris](https://sites.google.com/view/ellis-paris/): a network connecting researchers and institutions across the Paris AI ecosystem.

Former projects
---------------

* [Mokaplan](https://team.inria.fr/mokaplan/): a joint INRIA, CNRS and Paris-Dauphine research group on numerical calculus of variations.
* [Noria](../noria/): an ERC project advancing scalable optimal transport for imaging and machine learning.
* [Σ-Vision](../sigma-vision/): an ERC project on sparse models for natural image processing.
* [Imaging in Paris](http://imaging-in-paris.github.io): a monthly seminar on the mathematical foundations of imaging.
* [Chaire END](http://chaire-end.weebly.com/): a research chair on high-dimensional data processing.
* [GdR MIA](https://fadili.users.greyc.fr/mia/): a French research network for the mathematics of imaging.
* [Natimage](https://www.ceremade.dauphine.fr/~peyre/natimages/): an ANR project on adaptivity for natural images and textures.

Other initiatives

* [Data @ ENS](http://data-ens.github.io): a seminar on data science at ENS.
