I am a senior researcher at [CNRS](https://www.cnrs.fr/) working at the [Department of Mathematics and Applications (DMA)](https://www.math.ens.psl.eu/) of École Normale Supérieure. I am the director of the [ENS Center for Data Science](https://csd.ens.psl.eu/) and the [ELLIS Unit Paris](https://sites.google.com/view/ellis-paris/), and the scientific director of [normalesup.ai](https://normalesup.ai/).

I serve on the steering board of the [CNRS AISSAI Center](https://aissai.cnrs.fr/aissai/) and the management committee of the [PR[AI]RIE–PSAI AI Cluster](https://www.prairie-psai.fr/governance/). I am also in charge of artificial intelligence for [CNRS Mathematics (INSMI)](https://www.insmi.cnrs.fr/) and lead [WOLF](./wolf/), an ERC Advanced Grant project on Wasserstein flow learning for multi-omics.
