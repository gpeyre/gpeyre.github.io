This website uses [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/) to understand which
pages and articles are read. This is audience measurement, not advertising
tracking. The following notice concerns this analytics service; other resources
used by the website, such as fonts and mathematical typesetting, are separate.

## What is measured?

The analytics script sends the page's address and title, and the domain of an
external referring website when available. Search terms, URL query parameters,
URL fragments, and the path of the referring page are excluded. Click tracking
is disabled. Published articles and forthcoming previews have separate addresses
in the statistics.

GoatCounter also compiles statistics such as browser, device, screen size,
language, and approximate country. Its service processes the visitor's IP address
and browser information to receive requests and distinguish repeat visits. Its
[privacy policy](https://www.goatcounter.com/help/privacy) explains its temporary
in-memory deduplication and data retention. This does **not** mean that no IP
address is processed.

No analytics cookies are used, and this integration does not create a persistent
visitor identifier in browser storage. It checks an existing browser-local
GoatCounter opt-out setting, if present. The statistics are intended only to
understand readership of this website, not to build advertising profiles.

## Your preferences

This website does not load GoatCounter when the browser sends **Do Not Track**
or **Global Privacy Control**, or when a GoatCounter opt-out is stored in that
browser. If that saved preference cannot be checked, analytics is not loaded.
Local previews, redirect pages, editorial tools, and embedded numerical
experiments are excluded. Opening an experiment inside an article therefore
does not add another page view.
